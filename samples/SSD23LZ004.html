How to use this page

Every map is X-ray window counts from the Bruker M4 Tornado: brightness compares one element across this sample, not one element with another, and nothing here is a concentration.

Looking at the maps

  • Layer: keyed RGB composites first (each colour is one element, mixed colours mean more than one), then single elements, then K/Al and Na/Al ratio maps.
  • Opacity fades the map over the photo; black is transparent drops the dark background so the rock shows through.
  • Zoom: pinch, mouse wheel, or the fit / 2× / 4× buttons; drag to pan when zoomed in.
  • Tap a spot for its element fingerprint and a rule-of-thumb mineral suggestion. Check it against the hand sample.

Traverse

  • Press Traverse, then drag across a vein. The arrow is the positive end.
  • Drag the round end handles to change length and direction, the square handle to change width, the centre handle to move.
  • Pick elements with the chips (click a swatch to change a colour). Plot as display level, approximate counts, ratio to Al, or normalised to each element's maximum; stacked panels gives every element its own axis; log axis helps when one element dominates.
  • Download CSV saves the bins for Excel.

Region and ternary

  • Press Region, then drag a loop around an area. Its pixels appear on a ternary in the right-hand panel.
  • Choose the apices from the presets (K–Na–Ca, K–Al–Ca, K–Al–Si, Fe–S–Cu…) or the dropdowns.
  • Red markers are ideal minerals by molar atom fraction; because the axes are count ratios, expect clusters near, not on, the markers (light elements such as Na and Mg count low).
  • Tap a cluster on the ternary to light those pixels up on the map; tap empty space to clear.

Offline

Back on the library page, each suite has Save this suite for offline. Once saved, this page works without a connection. Traverses, regions and colours are kept while the browser tab is open.
